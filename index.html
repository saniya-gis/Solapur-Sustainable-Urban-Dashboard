Strategic Planning Actions

Recommendations derived from Solapur's heat and air quality data integrations.

1. Cool the hot zones first. A third of the city (33.7%) is 2 °C or more above average, and 6% is 5 °C or more. Start with those pixels: add tree cover, shaded streets and cool or reflective roofs. Map them to wards to set priority.

2. Protect the cool areas. About 26% of pixels are 2 °C or more below average (water, vegetation). Keep them as green and blue corridors and stop them being built over.

3. Keep air quality as is. NO₂, CO, O₃ and CH₄ are at normal or background levels in May 2025. Plan transit, walking and cycling now so traffic does not push NO₂ up as the city grows.

4. Check SO₂ at source. SO₂ is noisy (std is about 145% of the mean), so look at industrial clusters and power sources with finer data before setting rules.

5. Heat and air interact. Hot, still air raises ozone formation. Cooling hot zones helps air quality too, so plan green cover where heat and traffic overlap.

6. Monitor Continuously. Repeat both analyses every season and by ward, then compare the share of pixels at +2 °C or more. A falling share shows the plan is working.
